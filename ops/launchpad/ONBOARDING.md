# Client onboarding checklist (one-page website)

Track each item in `client_projects.assets` (true or false), so ULTRON shows what's missing.

1. **Business name**, exactly as customers know it
2. **Phone number** for the call buttons (and whether texts are OK)
3. **Email for form requests** (we test delivery before launch)
4. **Services**: 3–6 items, each with one plain sentence
5. **Service area**: cities and neighborhoods
6. **Hours**, and whether they offer emergency service (only if true)
7. **Logo**, if they have one (PNG or SVG)
8. **Photos**: 2–6 of real work, trucks or the team. Optional. Never stock photos passed off as theirs.
9. **License number**, if they want it shown (we never invent one)
10. **Domain**: do they own one, where is it registered, and who has the login? (DNS changes need the client's OK and the owner's approval)
11. **Reviews**: only real ones they choose, each with its public source link. Optional.

Deposit paid? Content complete? → status `in_production`, and the 3–5 business-day clock starts.
