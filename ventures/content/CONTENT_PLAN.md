## Rules that keep reach healthy
- **First 2 seconds:** the hook slide says the problem in under 10 words. It's already built into every carousel.
- **Keep the same topic per channel each day.** Don't post 5 times in an hour.
- **Captions end with one action:** "Save this", "Link in bio" or "Comment your niche". One ask per post.
- **AI label:** story videos must carry it. Carousels are real product pages and real prompts with no AI imagery, so no label is needed, but never say "made by hand".
- **No claims:** no sales numbers, earnings or results until they're real. When the first sales land, that becomes a post ("my first Etsy sale, here's the listing"), the proof the Skool offer needs.
