## The honesty rules (do not remove)
- **No income promises or earnings examples:** never say or imply buyers will make money, how much, or how fast. No screenshots of earnings, no "replace your job", no "passive income guaranteed". (FTC rules on earnings claims and business opportunities.)
- **Real status only:** say what is true today. You are testing these income streams now, and the results are early and unproven. That honesty is the selling point: you show the real system, including what doesn't work.
- **No fake buyers:** no fake testimonials, member counts, "spots left" countdowns or fake deadlines. The only deadline is the real pre-sale close date.
- **Disclose AI:** if a video uses an AI presenter or AI animation, it says so (the platform AI labels).
- **Delivery and refunds:** the masterclass is delivered by the date on the sales page, or every buyer gets a full refund.

1. **How I find income ideas with AI.** The research system: YouTube trend data, evidence before opinions, scoring ideas, killing bad ones fast.
2. **Digital products in a weekend.** Printable planners on Etsy and prompt packs on Gumroad: the real files, listings and prices, and how they were made.

6. **My real numbers (whatever they are by then).** An open look at what sold, what didn't, and what each test cost. No cherry-picking.
