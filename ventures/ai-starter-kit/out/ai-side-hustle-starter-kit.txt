AI SIDE HUSTLE STARTER KIT

30 copy-and-paste AI prompts to pick a small digital product, make it, list it and post about it in 7 days, plus a free AI tools list and a 7-day checklist. The same process we use for our own printables and prompt packs.

STARTER CONTEXT (paste once per chat)
----------------------------------------
You are my practical business coach. I'm starting a small digital product side project with AI tools.
Use only the facts I give you. If something is missing, ask me instead of guessing. Never promise income or results.
My skills and interests: [SKILLS AND INTERESTS, e.g. teaching, organizing, baking, fitness]
Time I have each week: [HOURS]
Budget: [AMOUNT, e.g. $0-$20]
Where I want to sell: [Etsy / Gumroad / not sure]
Who I'd like to help: [PEOPLE, e.g. busy parents, new teachers, small shop owners]
----------------------------------------

== 1. PICK YOUR IDEA ==

01. Ideas from what you already know
Using my skills and interests, list 15 small digital products I could make in a weekend (printables, checklists, templates, planners, prompt packs, guides). For each: who it's for, the problem it solves, and why my background helps. No income estimates.

02. Score the ideas
Score these ideas from 1-5 on: how easy to make in 2 days, how clear the buyer is, how easy to show in a photo or short video, and how much I'd enjoy making it: [PASTE IDEAS]. Show a table, then pick the top 3 and explain why in one line each.

03. Check demand yourself (no guessing)
I'll check demand myself. For my idea [IDEA], give me: 10 search phrases to type into Etsy, Gumroad Discover, Pinterest and YouTube; what to write down for each result (number of listings, review counts, prices I see, how recent); and how to read it. Do not tell me what the results will be.

04. Read my demand notes
Here is what I found when I searched: [PASTE NOTES: listings, review counts, prices, video views]. Using only these notes, tell me whether people seem to be buying or watching this, what the popular versions have in common, and one gap I could fill. Say clearly if the evidence is weak.

05. Find my angle
Popular products for [IDEA] look like this: [DESCRIBE TOP 3]. Suggest 5 ways to make mine different for [WHO I HELP] (niche, format, style, bundle, simpler). Pick the one that is quickest to make.

06. My 20-day plan
Make a 20-day plan to launch [PRODUCT] with [HOURS] hours a week and [BUDGET]. Days 1-7: make it. Days 8-10: list it. Days 11-20: post about it and review numbers. One small task per day, each under [MINUTES] minutes.


== 2. MAKE THE PRODUCT ==

07. Outline the product
Outline [PRODUCT] for [WHO I HELP]: page list (or section list), what goes on each page, and the one page that makes it worth buying. Keep it small enough to finish in 2 days.

08. Write the content
Write the content for page/section "[PAGE NAME]" of [PRODUCT]. Audience: [WHO]. Tone: [TONE]. Use plain words and short lines that fit on a printable page. Mark anything I need to check with [CHECK].

09. Printable layout brief
Describe a clean layout for a printable page called "[PAGE NAME]" that I can build in Canva or Google Docs: page size [US Letter / A4], sections, headings, how many writing lines, fonts that look good together, and 2 color options.

10. Prompt pack builder
I want to sell a prompt pack for [WHO]. List 25 tasks they do every week that AI can help with. Then write 5 example prompts with [BRACKETS] for their details, each telling the AI not to invent facts.

11. Quality check
Act as a picky buyer. Review this product content: [PASTE]. List anything confusing, missing, repeated or too long, and anything that sounds like a promise I can't keep. Then give me a fixed version of the 3 weakest parts.

12. Instructions page
Write a short 'How to use this' page for [PRODUCT]: how to print or open it, how to fill it in, and 3 tips. Under 150 words, friendly.


== 3. LIST IT ==

13. Title options
Write 10 listing titles for [PRODUCT] for [Etsy / Gumroad]. Put the words buyers would search first. Etsy titles under 140 characters. No 'best', no claims I can't prove.

14. Description
Write a listing description for [PRODUCT]: who it's for, the problem it solves, exactly what's included ([FILES, PAGES, SIZES]), how they get it (instant download), and what it is not. Short paragraphs and bullet points. No income or results promises.

15. Etsy tags
Give me 13 Etsy tags for [PRODUCT], each under 20 characters, mixing what it is, who it's for and when it's used. No repeated words from the title where a better phrase exists.

16. Pricing from my own research
Here are prices I saw for similar products: [PASTE PRICES]. Suggest a launch price for mine and explain in 3 lines. Use only these prices; if I gave too few, tell me to check more.

17. Listing photos shot list
Plan 5 listing images for [PRODUCT] using real pages from my file: cover, inside pages, what's included, size/format, and one 'in use' mockup. For each, say what text to put on it in under 8 words.

18. Buyer FAQ
Write 6 short FAQ answers for my listing: file type, printing, sizes, refunds for digital items ([MY POLICY]), editing, and using it on a tablet. Use only my facts: [FACTS].


== 4. POST ABOUT IT ==

19. Story-style short video script
Write a 15-second vertical video script in 3 beats: (1) a person struggling with [PROBLEM] says one line, (2) a friend shows them [PRODUCT] in one line, (3) the 'after' shot with one calm line. Natural speech, no income claims. Include on-screen text for each beat.

20. Captions
Write 3 short captions for a video about [PRODUCT] for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Each with a hook line, one sentence about what it is, 'link in bio', and 4 hashtags. If the video uses AI, add 'Made with AI'.

21. Pinterest pins
Write 5 Pinterest pin titles (under 100 characters) and descriptions (under 300 characters) for [PRODUCT] that answer what people search, like '[SEARCH PHRASE]'. Plain, helpful, no clickbait.

22. 7-day posting plan
Make a 7-day posting plan for [PRODUCT] across [PLATFORMS] with [MINUTES] minutes a day: what to post each day, the hook, and which product page to show.

23. Turn one video into five
I made this video: [DESCRIBE]. Give me 5 ways to reuse it (different hook, different first frame, a pin, a carousel, a 'how I made it' post) with the exact text for each.

24. Reply to comments
Write friendly replies to these comments on my post: [PASTE COMMENTS]. Answer questions honestly, point to the link only when they ask, and never promise results.


== 5. RUN IT LIKE AN EXPERIMENT ==

25. Set a target
Help me set a simple 14-day target for [PRODUCT] (for example 'first 3 sales' or '100 listing views'), what I'll count each day, and where I'll find each number in [Etsy Stats / Gumroad Analytics / app insights].

26. Day 7 check
Here are my numbers after 7 days: [VIEWS, CLICKS, FAVORITES, SALES PER PLATFORM]. Using only these, tell me where people drop off and the one change to try this week.

27. Day 14 decision
Here are my 14-day numbers: [NUMBERS]. Should I keep going, change the listing, change the product, or stop and try the next idea? Give your reasoning in 5 lines and say what extra data would change your answer.

28. Next product from the same buyers
My buyers for [PRODUCT] are [WHO]. Suggest 5 follow-up products or bundles for the same people, smallest first.

29. Weekly review
Ask me 6 quick questions to review my week (what I made, posted, learned, what flopped, numbers, energy). Then write a 3-line plan for next week.

30. Keep it honest
Read my listing and captions: [PASTE]. Flag anything that could read as an income promise, a fake result, missing AI disclosure, or a claim I can't back up, and rewrite those lines.

== 7-DAY CHECKLIST ==
[ ] Day 1: Pick your idea: run prompts 1-2, choose one product and who it's for.
[ ] Day 2: Check demand yourself (prompts 3-4): write down what you see, decide go or next idea.
[ ] Day 3: Outline and write the content (prompts 7-8).
[ ] Day 4: Build the pages in Canva or Docs and export a PDF (prompt 9). Run the quality check (prompt 11).
[ ] Day 5: Make 5 listing images from real pages (prompt 17). Write the title, description and tags (prompts 13-15).
[ ] Day 6: Publish the listing on Etsy or Gumroad. Write your first video script and captions (prompts 19-20).
[ ] Day 7: Post the first video and 5 pins. Set your 14-day target (prompt 25).

== FREE AI TOOLS ==
Plan and write
- ChatGPT (chatgpt.com): Idea lists, plans, product text, listing copy. Free plan available.
- Claude (claude.ai): Longer documents and careful editing. Free plan available.
- Gemini (gemini.google.com): A second opinion on ideas and research questions. Free with a Google account.
Make the product
- Canva (canva.com): Printable pages, covers and listing images. Free plan available.
- Google Docs / Slides: Simple PDFs: write in Docs, export as PDF. Free.
Sell it
- Gumroad (gumroad.com): Sell digital downloads with no monthly fee; Gumroad takes a fee per sale. Check the current fee on their pricing page.
- Etsy (etsy.com): Marketplace with buyers already searching. Charges a small listing fee plus fees per sale; check Etsy's current fees.
Get seen
- CapCut (capcut.com): Edit short vertical videos on your phone. Free plan available.
- Pinterest (business account): Pins keep sending visitors for months. Free.
- YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Instagram Reels: Free short-video reach. Label AI-made videos with each app's AI label.

This is a process, not a promise of income or results. Check AI output before you publish.
